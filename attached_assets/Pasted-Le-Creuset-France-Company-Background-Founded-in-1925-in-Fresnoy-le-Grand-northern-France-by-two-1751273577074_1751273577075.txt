Le Creuset - France
Company Background: Founded in 1925 in Fresnoy-le-Grand, northern France, by two Belgian industrialists: Armand Desaegher (casting specialist) and Octave Aubecq (enameling specialist). They pioneered enameled cast iron cookware, creating the first cocotte (French oven) that year. The signature “Volcanic” orange color was inspired by molten cast iron in the foundry molds. Le Creuset revolutionized cookware by bringing color to the kitchen and has become synonymous with premium enameled cast iron cookware worldwide.

De Buyer - France
Company Background: Founded in 1830 in the Vosges mountains of France as a metallurgy manufactory in Val-d’Ajol. Originally produced sheet metal and hand-crafted wrought iron utensils using local iron ore. The company has been family-owned for over 190 years, with Hervé de Buyer repositioning the brand toward high-end markets in 1988. Known for professional-grade cookware made from multiple materials including steel, stainless steel, copper, and coated aluminum.

Fissler - Germany
Company Background: Founded in 1845 by Carl Philipp Fissler in Idar-Oberstein, Germany. Started as a small tinsmith workshop producing cooking kettles. Became famous for innovations including the “Goulash Cannon” mobile field kitchen in the 19th century, the first aluminum pans for electric stoves in the 1920s, and the first pressure cooker with multi-setting control valve in the 1950s. Remains family-owned and is recognized for combining leading technology with traditional German craftsmanship.

WMF - Germany
Company Background: Founded in 1853 by Daniel Straub as a small metal-plating business in Geislingen, Germany. Merged with A. Ritter & Co. in 1880 to become Württembergische Metallwarenfabrik (WMF). By 1914, WMF had become one of Europe’s largest employers with over 6,000 workers. Pioneered stainless steel cookware production in 1927 with “Cromargan” stainless steel. Known for innovative design and premium German engineering in cookware and cutlery.

Samuel Groves - UK
Company Background: Established in 1817 in Birmingham, England, initially making brass candlesticks. Founded by Samuel Groves Sr., later succeeded by Samuel Groves Jr. in 1866. Gained prominence during WWII with government contracts for aluminum trays and military equipment. Introduced the iconic “Mermaid” brand in the 1950s, inspired by a statue found in an antique shop. Acquired by U Group in 2014, continuing the tradition of handcrafted British cookware manufacturing.

Solidteknics - Australia
Company Background: Founded in 2014 by mechanical engineer Mark J. Henry on Australia’s Sunshine Coast. Created to address environmental concerns about disposable synthetic-coated cookware filling landfills. Spent two decades in R&D to develop seamless one-piece wrought iron cookware from single sheets of Australian iron. Manufactures in Sydney using patented world-first innovations, offering centuries-long guarantees on their toxin-free, naturally seasoned cookware.

Tramontina - Brazil
Company Background: Founded in 1911 by Italian immigrant Valentin Tramontina in Carlos Barbosa, Rio Grande do Sul, Brazil. Started as a small blacksmith shop making horseshoes and pocket knives. After Valentin’s death in 1939, his wife Elisa took over, later passing to their son Ivo. Expanded internationally in 1986, establishing operations in Houston, Texas. Now Brazil’s largest cutlery company with over 22,000 products and presence in 120+ countries.

Staub - France
Company Background: Founded in 1974 by Francis Staub in Turckheim, Alsace, France. Started in a dormant artillery factory, initially producing only cocottes (Dutch ovens). Known for distinctive black matte enamel interior and innovative nub design on lids for self-basting. Acquired by Zwilling J.A. Henckels in 2008 but operates independently. Celebrated for combining French culinary tradition with modern innovation in enameled cast iron cookware.

Netherton Foundry - UK
Company Background: Founded in 2011 by Neil and Sue Currie in Shropshire, England, the birthplace of the Industrial Revolution. Skeppshult - Sweden
Company Background: Founded in 1906 in Sweden, producing handmade cast iron cookware using traditional methods. Located in an eco-friendly factory powered entirely by wind and hydropower. Each piece is hand-poured using unique sand molds that are used only once, making every product individually crafted. Known for ultra-lightweight cast iron innovations that are 40% lighter than traditional cast iron while maintaining superior performance. Represents over a century of Swedish craftsmanship and environmental responsibility.

Riess - Austria
Company Background: Family business dating back to 1550 when a pan smithy was founded in Ybbsitz, Lower Austria. Formally established in the early 1800s, began producing iconic enameled cookware in 1922. Uses a CO2-neutral production process powered by their own hydraulic power station since the 1920s. Now in its 11th generation of family ownership, known for distinctive white enamelware and pastel collections that evoke Austrian tradition.

Emile Henry - France
Company Background: Founded in 1850 by potter Jacques Henry in Marcigny, Burgundy, France. Started with a small workshop using manual wheels and wood-fired kilns. The company name honors Emile Henry, who took over in 1922. Uses proprietary Burgundy clay from mineral-rich limestone soils that impart unique cooking properties. Still family-owned and operated, now in its fifth generation, creating ceramic cookware that’s freezer-to-oven safe.

Paderno - Canada
Company Background: The Canadian Paderno plant opened in 1979 on Prince Edward Island as part of Paderno Italy’s North American operations. In 1986, local businessman Jim Casey bought the factory and retained rights to use the Paderno name in Canada. Now owned by Casey’s son Tim, the facility produces up to 220,000 pots annually. Known for tri-ply stainless steel construction with magnetic bases for induction compatibility.

Zebra - Thailand
Company Background: Founded in 1966 by Mr. Satien Youngvanich, originally as “Shoom Silp Industries Limited Partnership” for welding commercial stainless steel equipment. First stainless steel spoon production began in 1968, becoming Thailand’s first stainless steel kitchenware manufacturer. Company moved to current Rayong province location in 1988, covering over 250 hectares. Certified with international quality standards ISO 9001:2008 and environmental management ISO 14001:2004.

Demeyere - Belgium
Company Background: Founded in 1908 by André Demeyere in Herentals, Belgium, as a small metalworking workshop. Originally made coffee pots and ashtrays, transitioning to stainless steel cookware in 1967 under Maurits Emmanuel’s leadership. Pioneered induction-compatible cookware before induction cooktops became mainstream. Acquired by Zwilling J.A. Henckels in 2008 but maintains Belgian production and family heritage. Known for innovative 7-ply construction and InductoSeal base technology.

Skottsberg - Netherlands
Company Background: Founded in 2019 by the Wegter family company as a Dutch sustainable cookware brand.
