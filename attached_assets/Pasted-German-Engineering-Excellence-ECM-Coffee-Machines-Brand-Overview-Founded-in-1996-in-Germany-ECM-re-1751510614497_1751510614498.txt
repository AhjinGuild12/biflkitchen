Italian Heritage and Tradition
La Pavoni
Brand Overview: Founded in 1905 by Desiderio Pavoni in Milan, La Pavoni created the first espresso coffee machine called “Ideale” and pioneered the espresso industry. The company launched the world’s first domestic electric espresso machine, the Europiccola, in 1961.
Coffee Making Philosophy: La Pavoni maintains true Italian tradition through artistic excellence in design, producing reliable espresso machines for traditional Italian espressos and cappuccinos.
